"Capturing the moments that matter — from the final out to the closing curtain."

Lyndsey Lou Photography specializes in on-site photography for sports leagues, dance recitals, and community events across the Jacksonville, Florida area. Based in Baldwin, FL, Lyndsey brings the studio experience directly to your field, gym, or stage — so no one misses a shot.

From Elm Street Little League to Hilliard Parks & Recreation, Lyndsey has built long-term relationships with local organizations that trust her to document their seasons, celebrations, and milestone moments.
