02 — The Experience

More Than a Photo Shoot

En Flor Boudoir is a safe, celebratory space built for every body. Each session is a full-service experience — from custom hair and makeup to the final gallery — designed around you and no one else.

Whether you're booking as a gift, a personal milestone, or simply because you deserve it, every detail is tailored to how you want to feel: powerful, tender, radiant, or all three at once.

03 — Services

What We Offer

I

In-Studio Sessions

Fully lit, professionally styled studio environment in Coconut Creek. Wardrobe styling available. Includes hair and makeup by our on-call artists.

II

On Location

Your home, a boutique hotel, or a curated venue of your choice. We bring the studio to you — lights, styling, and all. Serving all of Broward County.

Bridal Boudoir

The ultimate gift to yourself — or a private album for your partner. Timed around your wedding date for the most memorable portraits of your life.
