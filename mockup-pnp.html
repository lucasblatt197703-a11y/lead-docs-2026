About
Mark Weinstock

"You just say when and where and he is there."

Based in Lake Worth, FL, Mark Weinstock has spent over 15 years building PNP Photography Inc. into one of South Florida's most trusted names in wedding, event, and portrait photography. Incorporated in 2009, the studio serves Palm Beach, Broward, and Miami-Dade counties.

Rooted in a photojournalistic approach, Mark's style blends documentary instinct with classic portraiture — capturing the authentic moments that define each occasion, from intimate ceremonies to large-scale events.
