About

Alese
Monahan

Mother, artist, problem solver — and photographer. Alese Monahan began her photography journey in high school, apprenticing under a working photographer before the craft was even a career path. What started as a volunteer gig taking preschool class photos became a calling.

"I take and print over 1,000 photos of my family every year. It wasn't until I volunteered to take the class photos that I realized I was meant for this."

Working across family sessions, weddings, school photography, dance recitals, and senior portraits, My Family Photography, LLC brings warmth and intention to every frame.
