About the Artist

"Born and raised in Central Florida — the light here is something you can't invent."

T.M. Burris — "Mrs. B" — lives in a small cabin on the edge of the Ocala National Forest in Silver Springs, Florida. Her subtropical landscapes are painted from life and from her own DSLR photography, taken on flat boat trips, hikes, and road trips through the state she has called home her entire life.

A career-pivot artist who returned to school in her fifties to earn a BFA in Graphic Design, Burris brings a designer's eye for composition to every canvas board. Her work is available in 9×12 and 8×10 formats — portable, intimate, made to live on real walls.
