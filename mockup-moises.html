About

"Working across metal, ink, and henna — each discipline informs the others."

Moises Orozco is a Tucson-based multi-disciplinary artist working across metal fabrication, illustration, and henna artistry. With roots in blacksmithing and a background spanning custom sculpture to intricate body art, his practice bridges industrial craft and fine detail work. Each piece — whether forged in steel or drawn on skin — is made to order, made by hand.
