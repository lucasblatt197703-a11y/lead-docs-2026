About the Artist

Photographer &
Intermedia Artist

North Carolina native Liz Lasine is a photographer, intermedia artist, and graphic designer based in Gainesville, Florida. Her practice spans digital photography, experimental darkroom processes, mixed-media collage, and graphic design — each discipline informing the others. Her intermedia work interrogates the photographic image through techniques such as mordançage, chromoskedasic Sabattier, and acrylic intervention. Select works available for purchase.
