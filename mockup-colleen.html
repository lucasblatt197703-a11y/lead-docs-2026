Colleen Kydd

Fort Myers, FL · Southwest Florida

Colleen Kydd is a Fort Myers-based photographer specializing in family portraits, special events, and golden hour sunset sessions throughout Southwest Florida. Her work prioritizes authenticity — putting people at ease so the real moments shine through. Drawn to the rich, vibrant colors of the SWFL coast, her photography carries a distinctive warmth: bright yet moody, emotional and dreamy.
