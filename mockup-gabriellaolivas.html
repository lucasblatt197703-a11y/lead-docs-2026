About

Gabriella Nicole has been making photographs since age ten — from a silver point-and-shoot to a full professional kit, the impulse to turn life into art has never wavered. Based in El Paso, Texas, she works across portraits, engagements, maternity, and family sessions.

Owner and operator of Olivas Photography & Design, Gabriella brings an artist's eye to every booking — treating each session as its own visual story, not a formula.

"I wanted to create art through photography for the rest of my life."
