02 — About

The artist
behind the work

Cindee Tabares works across painting and photography from her base in Coral Gables, Miami. Her practice draws on the textures of South Florida life — light off water, the geometry of urban space, the intimacy of portraiture. By day a communications and marketing professional; by night and weekend, a maker of images that need no brief.

"My free time is spent chasing what can't be scheduled — the painting, the photograph, the unrepeatable moment."
