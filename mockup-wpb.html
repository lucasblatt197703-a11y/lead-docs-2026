About

Angelo Schillaci Sr. is a Jupiter-based photographer whose work spans the full breadth of South Florida's natural and human landscape. Rooted in the light and terrain of the Treasure Coast, his images move from intimate nature studies to wide-open seascapes, product work, and portraiture.

"Local Photography Authority for social events, deals, groups, shops & photographers."

Working under the WPB Photography banner since 2019, Angelo documents the distinctive light and ecology of Jupiter, West Palm Beach, and the surrounding Treasure Coast — offering fine art prints, commercial work, and event coverage.
