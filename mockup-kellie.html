About Kellie

"This is not a job for me — this is a passion I never get tired with."

With over two decades behind the lens, Kellie Marie has built her reputation on one simple belief: love stories deserve to be told authentically. Recently relocated to Tampa Bay, she brings her deep experience from North Carolina and South Carolina to the Florida wedding market — along with the warmth and candor that have made her a trusted name in the industry.

From garden ceremonies along the Hillsborough River to destination elopements across the coast, Kellie invests her whole heart in every session. Her work spans weddings, engagements, quinceañeras, and events — all captured with her signature natural-light style.
