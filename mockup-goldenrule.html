The Photographer Behind the Lens

"Named for the golden ratio — and for the way every client deserves to be treated."

Michelle photographs small businesses, families, and wildlife across Polk County using only natural light in beautiful outdoor settings. Based in Lake Wales, she brings a patient, unhurried approach to every session.

Golden Rule Photography was built on a simple belief: great images shouldn't come with hidden costs. No sitting fees. No pressure. Just honest work done right.

Serving Lake Wales, Lakeland, Winter Haven, and all surrounding areas in central Florida.
