About Muddy Mama Photography

We chase the golden hour and don't mind getting a little muddy

Muddy Mama Photography is a Fort Myers-based family photography studio specializing in outdoor lifestyle sessions across Southwest Florida. The work celebrates real family moments — kids running barefoot, siblings splashing in puddles, parents laughing with their eyes closed.

"The best portraits happen when everyone forgets the camera is there."

Serving Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples, and the surrounding Gulf Coast communities — from golden hour beach sessions to nature trail adventures.
