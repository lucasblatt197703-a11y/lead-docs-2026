About the Artist

Tactile, layered,
unmistakably her own

Born in Zagreb, Croatia and rooted in Orlando, Florida, Mirjana Novak Cesar works across fiber art, mixed media painting, soft sculpture, and art jewelry — disciplines united by an insistence on texture as meaning. Her Croatian-inspired landscapes and nature-driven canvases are built up in layers: thread, fiber, acrylic, and found material pressed into surfaces that reward close looking.

A resident artist at Faith Arts Village Orlando (Studio #238), Mirjana's work is represented at Florida CraftArt Gallery in St. Petersburg and featured in numerous issues of Stampington & Company magazines. Her Etsy shop — active since 2008 — carries wearable fiber art and OOAK jewelry to collectors across the country.

"The work should feel like something — not just look like it."
