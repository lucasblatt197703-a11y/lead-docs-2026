About the Photographer

Angela DiBella

Based in Tallahassee, Florida, Angela DiBella has built her practice around one simple belief: that every family deserves photography that actually looks like them. A self-taught photographer who fell in love with film in high school, she now specializes in newborn, family, and birth photography — sessions with no time limits and no pressure.

"Capturing the moments of today that will wow your heart tomorrow."

Angela is the only photographer in Tallahassee who delivers all best images — professionally edited and yours to keep. From Fresh 48 birth sessions to wedding collections, she brings the same unhurried, warm presence to every shoot.
