About

"Working in oils, I try to capture something that lasts — a pet, a moment, a face that someone loves."

Sandy Clifton is a Castle Rock, Colorado oil painting artist working in wildlife, portraiture, still life, and landscape. Using the highest quality paints and linen canvases, each commissioned piece is created to be a true heirloom — artwork that will outlast its creator and stay meaningful for generations.

Commissions are open for pets, portraits, and still life. Sandy works with clients across the country, using client photos and careful consultation to ensure every piece captures exactly what matters most.
