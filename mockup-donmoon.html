The Artist

Working in oil and enamel on reclaimed industrial aluminum, Don Moon creates abstract works rooted in materiality and process. Based in St. Petersburg, Florida, Moon's practice is defined by the tension between control and accident — the surface itself becomes a collaborator.

Each piece evolves through layers of addition and subtraction, with the painter moving across the surface, responding intuitively to color, form, and the particular resistance of metal. The industrial substrate — aluminum salvaged and repurposed — carries its own history into every work.
