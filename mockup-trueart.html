Owner and creative force behind True A.R.T., Alex Cartagena has built one of Miami Beach's most original tattoo experiences. Trained extensively in fine art, Alex specializes in realism, freehand composition, and color work — with the ability to bring any concept to life on skin.

At True A.R.T., the atmosphere is designed to feel nothing like your average shop. Comfortable, collaborative, and entirely focused on delivering a tattoo you'll be proud to carry for life.
