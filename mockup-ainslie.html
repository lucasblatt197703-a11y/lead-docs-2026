About

Meet Jennifer

Jennifer Clark is the photographer behind Ainslie Images Photography, a studio she founded in Cape Coral, Florida in 2015. Specializing in newborn, family, maternity, and portrait work, Jennifer has spent over a decade creating images that families hold on to for a lifetime. Her studio at 19 Del Prado Blvd N offers clients a comfortable, fully-equipped space for any session.

"Every session is a chance to capture something real — something families will want to frame and pass down."

"The best images aren't posed — they're the ones that happen between poses."

Jennifer Clark — Ainslie Images Photography
