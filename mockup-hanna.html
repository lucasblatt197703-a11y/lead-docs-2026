The art of
being seen

Hannah Pannell is a portrait photographer based in Merritt Island, Florida. With a camera in hand since age twelve, she builds images around stillness and truth — setting up scenes that unfold into stories, one frame at a time.

"I've always been struck by photography — the way it holds a moment still."

Working across beach environments, studio spaces, and natural landscapes of the Space Coast, Hannah brings the same unhurried attention to every subject — whether it's a senior portrait or a family's afternoon in the sun.
