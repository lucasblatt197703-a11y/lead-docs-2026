The Photographer Behind the Lens

"People look their best when they are genuinely enjoying each moment of their day."

Grace Jicha is a Central Florida–based wedding and elopement photographer specializing in candid, unposed imagery that captures love as it actually feels. Drawing on a background in journalism, her work prioritizes authentic storytelling over picture-perfect poses.

Since her first paid shoot in 2016, Grace has photographed weddings and elopements across Florida and beyond — from intimate beachside ceremonies to full-scale venue celebrations. Her images have been featured in Marry Me Tampa Bay, one of the region's leading wedding publications.
