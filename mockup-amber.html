About Amber

"What inspires me is you — your energy, your heart, your presence. You are the magic."

Amber Sycz is a South Florida beach photographer specializing in couples, families, engagements, and maternity sessions. Holding a BFA from the University of Florida, she has always been drawn to photographing real connection in beautiful, natural light.

Working on select beaches across Pompano Beach, Deerfield Beach, Boca Raton, Lauderdale by the Sea, and beyond — her style is true to life with a touch of artistry, capturing moments that feel effortless, dreamy, and timeless.
