About the Photographer

Where every frame holds something real

Savannah Ponce is a Miami-based portrait and wedding photographer whose work is grounded in genuine connection. She grew up in a family of creatives — her mother a photographer, her father a storyteller — and found her own voice through the lens.

"Photography has been my outlet — like therapy — while also being fun."

Working with models, couples, families, and individuals across South Florida and beyond, Savannah brings warmth and intention to every session.
