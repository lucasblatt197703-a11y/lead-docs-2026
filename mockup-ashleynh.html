02 — The Photographer

Light, place,
and natural truth.

Ashley Howard is a Volusia County–based photographer working across portraiture, landscape, wedding, and wildlife. A graduate of the University of North Florida with a BFA in Photography, her work is rooted in natural light and the distinct character of Florida's coastline.

"Her work has been displayed in galleries in Jacksonville — CoRK Arts District, Monroe Gallery, and UNF's Gallery of Art."

Serving Ponce Inlet, Daytona Beach, New Smyrna Beach, Port Orange, Ormond Beach, and surrounding Volusia County areas.
