02  ·  The Photographer

Summer
Dawn Urso

Based in Tampa, Summer Dawn Urso is an award-winning photographer specializing in photojournalistic weddings, portraiture, and boudoir. With over a decade behind the lens, her approach is documentary at its core — capturing the unguarded, the fleeting, the real.

"You don't make a photograph just with a camera. You bring to the act of photography all the pictures you have seen, the books you have read, the music you have heard, the people you have loved."

Her work spans weddings, portraits, maternity, newborns, boudoir, and events — always with an eye for the moment that only happens once.
