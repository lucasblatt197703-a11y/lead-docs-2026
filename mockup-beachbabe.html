02 — About the Photographer

Meet
Lacey

A digital photographer based on Florida's Space Coast, Lacey built Beach Babe Photography around a single belief: every ordinary moment deserves to be remembered beautifully. Raised on the island of Oahu and rooted in Cocoa since 2009, she brings a warm, unhurried approach to every session — whether it's a newborn's first hours or a family's last summer before college.

"Time passes quickly and as it does our memories tend to fade."
