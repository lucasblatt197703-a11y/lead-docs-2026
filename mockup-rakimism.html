The Photographer

Wesly
Edwards

Working under the name Rakimism, Wesly Edwards is a visual storyteller rooted in Jacksonville, FL. His practice spans lifestyle, branding, and creative portraiture — each session shaped by intentionality, emotional clarity, and a sense of lived-in humanity.

Studio-based at CoRK Arts District, Studio 7, his work has been exhibited at MOCA Jacksonville's Triennial and serves individuals, entrepreneurs, and brands across Jacksonville, Los Angeles, New York, and Atlanta.
