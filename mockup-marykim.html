About

Visual Artist
& Storyteller

Based in Pensacola, Florida, Mary Kim is a photographer and designer whose work bridges documentary observation with considered visual craft. A graduate of the University of the South at Sewanee, she brings both academic rigor and an eye honed through years of practice to every project.

Working across photography and graphic design, her practice explores how images and visual systems can carry emotional weight — whether through a single frame or a complete brand identity.
