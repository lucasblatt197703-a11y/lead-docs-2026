About

Amy & the
art of memory

With over eleven years behind the lens, Amy has built Eddie Mae Photography on one simple truth: the smallest moments matter most. Specializing in newborn, maternity, family, and milestone sessions, she works from a home studio in Northwood, ND — and travels when the moment calls for it.

A mother of five with a grandchild on the way, Amy photographs what she knows: time passes fast, and these images are how you hold it still.
