Meet the Photographer

Hi, I'm Cara.

Family and lifestyle photographer based in Maitland, FL. I established CLD Photography in 2015 out of a love for capturing life's most important moments — from new babies to milestone events, family gatherings to corporate headshots.

A mother of three myself, I understand the value of authentic, unhurried sessions where everyone feels comfortable. My daughter Gavriella often joins as a second shooter, bringing an extra set of eyes and energy to every session.

When we're not behind a camera, you'll find us kayaking, camping, or being beach bums somewhere along the Florida coast.
