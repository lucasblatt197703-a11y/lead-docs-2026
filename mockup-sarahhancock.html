02 — About

A photographer who captures life as it actually feels

Based along the northeast Florida coast, Sarah Hancock specializes in portrait, maternity, newborn, and family photography — sessions designed around real moments, warm light, and unhurried connection. Working across Palm Coast, Flagler Beach, Ormond Beach, and Daytona Beach, each session is tailored to the people in front of the lens.

Sessions begin at $125, with packages ranging to $400 for comprehensive galleries. Investment pricing available on request.
