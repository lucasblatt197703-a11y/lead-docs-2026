03 About
"I love seeing stacks of photographs all over my house — that's how much I love what I do."

Based in Jacksonville, Florida, Carrie Scruggs has spent nearly two decades documenting the quiet, real moments that define family life. Her work has appeared in national retail catalogs for IKEA and Walmart, and she serves communities across Saint Johns, Jacksonville, Switzerland, and Saint Augustine.

A film photographer at heart, Scruggs brings an unhurried, tactile sensibility to every session — the belief that the best photographs are the ones you'll still be looking at in thirty years.
