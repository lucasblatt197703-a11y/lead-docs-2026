Behind the Door

"Challenging patriarchal norms through a Victorian and Pictorial aesthetic — exploring sexuality, power, death of self, and feminine rebirth."

Taylor-Hicks's thesis work draws on her own experiences within the suffocating confines of a failing marriage, using references to the cultural restrictions imposed on Victorian women. Binary oppositions — light versus dark, life versus death, birth and rebirth — form the structural and conceptual backbone of the series.

About

Thirty years behind the lens. A practice rooted in the body, memory, and power.

Working in fine art photography with a concentration in pictorial and Victorian aesthetics, Milisa Taylor-Hicks earned her MFA from Savannah College of Art and Design in 2015. Her practice spans conceptual portraiture, alternative process, and experimental digital art.

Currently teaching Advanced Placement and AICE Photography at Clay County School District, Fleming Island, FL. Adjunct faculty alumni, University of Florida. Member, Society for Photographic Education.

2015 MFA Photography — Savannah College of Art and Design. Educators Scholarship & Alumni Grant recipient. GPA 4.0.
