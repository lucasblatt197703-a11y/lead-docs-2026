02 · About

Behind the
Lens

Gioia Doukas is a Florida-based photographer with a gift for capturing the fleeting, irreplaceable moments of early life. Working primarily with newborns, young families, and milestone portraits, her approach is unhurried and warm — creating space for genuine emotion rather than posed perfection.

Based throughout Florida and available for travel, Gioia brings her signature style — soft natural light, warm tones, and a genuine connection with her subjects — to every session. Her work reflects a belief that the best photographs feel like memory, not performance.
