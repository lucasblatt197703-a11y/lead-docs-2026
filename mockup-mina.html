Underwater Fashion — Where Couture Meets the Deep

Shooting in South Florida pools and open ocean, Mina captures the surreal moment when garment, model, and water become one. Fabric billows, hair suspends, and gravity disappears — creating images that exist nowhere else in photography.

Each session requires precise coordination of light, current, and breath. The result is something between fashion editorial and fine art.

03

About

"The water transforms everything — it makes the ordinary surreal and the familiar mysterious."

Mina Cassandra Kuhn began photographing underwater nature in 1997, drawn to the Caribbean's reefs and the light that filters through clear water. That curiosity evolved into a singular practice at the intersection of underwater photography and high fashion.

After studying fashion photography at The International Center of Photography and the School of Visual Arts in New York City, Mina relocated to Miami in 2005 — finding in South Florida the perfect environment for her signature work. The warm ocean, strong equatorial light, and Miami's vibrant creative community all inform her aesthetic.

Her work spans underwater portraits, editorial fashion, nature photography, and lifestyle — each series carrying the same unhurried, precise eye.
