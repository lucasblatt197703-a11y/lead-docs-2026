The Photographer

Leslie Jean Wengler is a fine art photographer based in Ocala, Florida, working primarily through the intimate language of the macro lens. Her work invites the viewer into a botanical universe that flourishes inside ordinary bushes, grass blades, and garden beds — where pistils, petals, stamens, and dewdrops share real estate in a colorful, alternate world.

"I knew I wanted to be a photographer since I was 9, but most of my inspiration comes from the written word — John Keats, Ralph Waldo Emerson, Walt Whitman."

A Ringling College of Art and Design alumna, Wengler returned to her lens after years of ministry work to debut her first solo exhibition — Flora: A State of Whim — at NOMA Gallery, Ocala. Her work has since been selected for display in the Florida State Capitol.
