04 — About

The Studio
Behind the Work

C3 Art LLC is the creative studio of Charles Releford III — a Miami-based realist graphite artist originally from Griffin, GA. Specializing in rendering realistic life-forms and objects with exceptional detail, Charles has built a full-service creative practice spanning fine art, logo design, and custom commissions.

"Art that starts conversations and lives on walls for generations."

From intimate portrait commissions to commercial brand identities, C3 Art operates at the intersection of fine art and applied design — a rare combination in the Miami creative scene.
