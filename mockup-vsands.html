About the Artist

Valerie Sands

A South Florida photographer with over 15 years behind the lens, Valerie Sands works across portraits, beauty, corporate, and event photography throughout Broward, Dade, and Palm Beach counties. Her work is built on a foundation of precision lighting, genuine connection with subjects, and an eye for the moments that matter.

"Photography is the art of making the unseen visible — the quiet strength, the real emotion, the story behind the face."

Also an experienced copywriter, Valerie brings a rare dual perspective to visual storytelling — understanding not just how to capture an image, but how to make it communicate.
