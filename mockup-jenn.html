About Jenn

Deltona's Specialist in Life's Gentlest Moments

"Every baby, every family, every pet — they each deserve a session as unhurried and care-filled as the love they carry."

Jenn Koss has been photographing the families of Central Florida since 2009, building a reputation for patience, warmth, and an instinct for the moments that parents will treasure for decades. Specializing in newborns — those first fragile days of life — Jenn's approach is calm, unhurried, and deeply personal.

Based in Deltona, FL, and serving the greater Orlando and Daytona corridor, Jenn brings professional-grade expertise to every session. Her work spans newborn portraits, family lifestyle photography, maternity, and pet photography.
