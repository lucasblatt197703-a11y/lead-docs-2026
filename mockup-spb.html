Based in West Palm Beach, FL, Shamarla Parks-Bullard founded SPB Photography with a simple belief — that great photography should feel joyful, not stressful. Specializing in weddings, events, and portrait sessions across South Florida, she brings a warm and people-first approach to every shoot. Her work is driven by genuine connection with the people she photographs.
