About

The Woman
Behind the Lens

Kimber Upayaso is a natural light photographer working across Northeast Florida and Southeast Georgia, with a particular love for the golden coast light of Fernandina Beach and Amelia Island.

Specializing in weddings, newborns, and family portraits, Kimber's approach is simple: put people at ease, find the real moments, and let the light do the heavy lifting. Her work has been described as warm, honest, and deeply personal.

Based in the Kingsland, GA area and available for travel throughout the region, she brings that same unhurried, careful eye to every session — whether it's a two-hour family beach session or a full wedding day.
