02 — About

The Artist

"Every woman deserves to see herself the way I see her through my lens."

Kyle Martiny is the photographer behind Rumi Grey Images — a Jacksonville-based portrait studio specializing in boudoir, bridal, and intimate portraiture. With a background in outdoor natural light photography and an eye for warmth and authenticity, Kyle creates images that feel both timeless and deeply personal.

Serving Jacksonville, Ponte Vedra Beach, and all of Northeast Florida, Rumi Grey Images is the studio of choice for women who want to feel extraordinary in front of the camera.
