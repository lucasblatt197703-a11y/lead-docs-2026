About the Artist

Casey J Rhodes is a Florida-based artist working across pencil, graphite, watercolor, acrylic, and oil. Drawing from pop culture, sports, cinema, and the natural landscape, Rhodes brings a distinctive hand to every subject — from sweeping landscape studies to intricate character renderings.

"Art rooted in the things that matter — the films, the games, the landscapes, the moments."

Each piece in the collection reflects an ongoing curiosity about how light, texture, and form translate across media. Whether working in graphite or oil on canvas, the result is work that is both technically grounded and deeply personal.
