About

The Photographer

Carolina Irais is a Central Florida elopement and wedding photographer whose approach is equal parts documentary and cinematic — she finds the unguarded glance, the quiet moment between vows, the laugh that catches everyone off guard. Born in Puerto Rico, based in Orlando, she photographs couples across Florida and beyond.

"I want you to look back at these images in twenty years and feel exactly what you felt that day."
